Your Calm Corner in Colombo Fort

Kafénio is a speciality coffee house that yearns to be savoured — slowly, one cup at a time

In Greek, a kafenío is the neighbourhood coffee house — the place you stop on the way somewhere else, and somehow stay. Ours sits on Hospital Street: warm timber, soft rattan, a long rippled wall washed in light and the steady hum of the grinder.
